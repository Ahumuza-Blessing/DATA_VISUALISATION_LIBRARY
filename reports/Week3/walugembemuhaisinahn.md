 Project Overview and Introduction
 Introduction		
Graphs and charts are commonly used to present data in a way that is easier to understand. They help us see different patterns and changes in the data. However, sometimes it can be difficult to understand what is happening at a particular point on a graph, especially when there is a sudden increase, decrease, or an unusual result.
Annotations can help solve this problem. They are notes, labels, or arrows added to a graph to explain certain points or show important information. They help the reader understand the graph better without having to guess what a particular result means.
In this project, I will be working on adding an annotation feature to the C++ Data Visualization Library. The feature will allow users to add text and labels to specific points on a graph. This will help them explain important information and make their graphs easier to understand.
